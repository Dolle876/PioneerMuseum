Welcome to the interactive virtual museum Pioneers of Space Exploration.

A museum is an institution dedicated to preserving and showcasing humanity’s historical and cultural heritage. The history of space exploration is part of this heritage; however, since many of the key artifacts, the stars of this history, are far away from home (located on the surfaces or in the orbits of other celestial bodies) and cannot be brought back for showcasing, we have created this interactive virtual museum.

Here, you can explore NASA artifacts that carried out missions on the Moon, Mars, and other celestial bodies, as well as those that remain in orbit—all of which have deepened our understanding of both our solar system and deep space.

Who We Are

This museum is the creation of the team Atenea G12, a participant in the 2026 edition of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, venue Puerto Madryn, Argentina. It is part of the "Abandoned but not Forgotten" challenge, which aims to make information about artifacts that have completed space exploration missions accessible to the general public in an engaging and enjoyable way, while also sparking interest among those who might be drawn to this subject. Our team’s name honors the Atenea CubeSat—developed in our country and recently deployed during the Artemis mission on its journey to the Moon.
